## How to sign

When You open a pull request, CLA Assistant (cla-assistant.io) comments on it with a link. Follow
the link, sign in with GitHub and accept the Agreement there; the pull request's CLA check turns
green. You sign once: later pull requests are checked against Your signature, until the text of this
Agreement changes, when CLA Assistant asks You to sign the new version on Your next pull request.
